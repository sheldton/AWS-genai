AnyCompany Insurance - Motor Policy Wording MP-2026

Effective 1 January 2026. This wording applies to every comprehensive motor policy issued or renewed by AnyCompany Insurance on or after that date. Read it together with your certificate of motor insurance, which lists the insured vehicle, the named drivers and any add-ons you bought.

Section 1 - Definitions

Vehicle means the car shown in your certificate, its standard accessories and fitted audio and navigation equipment.

Named driver means a person listed in your certificate who may drive the vehicle.

Flood damage means damage to the vehicle caused by water from a river, the sea, heavy rain or a burst water main, including water that enters the engine.

Write-off means that the cost of repairing the vehicle is more than 60 percent of its market value.

Section 2 - What is covered

We cover loss of or damage to your vehicle caused by the following events.

2.1 Accident, including a collision with another vehicle, an animal or a fixed object.

2.2 Fire, lightning and explosion.

2.3 Theft or attempted theft of the vehicle, provided it was locked and the keys were not left in or on it.

2.4 Flood damage and storm damage. Flood is covered for vehicles under this policy without an add-on. Do not start the engine of a car that has stood in water - engine damage caused by starting a flooded engine is not covered.

2.5 Windscreen and window glass, including the sunroof. A repaired chip does not affect your no-claims discount.

2.6 Personal belongings in the vehicle up to 300 USD per claim, if they are stolen from a locked vehicle or damaged in an accident.

Section 3 - What is not covered

3.1 Damage while the vehicle is driven by a person who is not a named driver, or by a driver without a valid licence.

3.2 Mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, and wear and tear of tyres and brakes.

3.3 Damage while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

3.4 Use of the vehicle for racing, track days or paid delivery work.

3.5 Loss of value of the vehicle after a repair.

Section 4 - Deductibles

The deductible is the part of each motor claim that you pay yourself:

  Cause of damage                                  Deductible per claim (USD)
  Accident (Section 2.1)                           600
  Theft of the vehicle (Section 2.3)               400
  Flood and storm (Section 2.4)                    350
  Windscreen repair (Section 2.5)                  0
  Windscreen replacement (Section 2.5)             150
  Young driver under 25, added to any of the above 300

Section 5 - How to make a motor claim

5.1 Report an accident or theft to us within 14 days, by phone or in the AnyCompany app.

5.2 For theft, report the loss to the police within 24 hours and give us the crime reference number.

5.3 Do not admit fault to the other driver. Exchange names, addresses, registration numbers and insurer details, and take photos of the scene.

5.4 We choose the repairer. If you use your own garage, we pay at most what our approved repairer would have charged.

Section 6 - Courtesy car

While your vehicle is repaired by our approved repairer we provide a small courtesy car for up to 14 days. After a theft or a write-off we provide a hire car for up to 21 days or until we settle the claim, whichever is earlier.

Section 7 - Settlement

7.1 If the vehicle is a write-off we pay its market value just before the loss, minus the deductible.

7.2 We pay within 10 business days of agreeing the settlement amount with you. If there is a loan on the vehicle, we pay the lender first.
