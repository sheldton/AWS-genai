AnyCompany Insurance - Home Policy Wording HP-2026

Effective 1 January 2026. This wording applies to every home policy issued or renewed by AnyCompany Insurance on or after that date. Read it together with your policy schedule, which lists your sum insured, your address and any add-ons you bought.

Section 1 - Definitions

Buildings means the structure of your home, its permanent fixtures and fittings, fitted kitchens and bathrooms, fixed glass in windows and doors, and outbuildings on the same plot.

Contents means household goods and personal belongings that you own and that are kept in your home, including furniture, carpets that are not fitted, electronics, clothing and jewelry.

Escape of water means water that leaks suddenly and accidentally from a fixed water tank, pipe, appliance or heating system inside your home.

Flood means water that enters your home from outside, for example from a river, the sea, a lake, rain run-off or a blocked public drain.

Storm means a period of violent weather with wind speeds of at least 89 km/h, or torrential rain of at least 25 mm per hour, or hail or snow heavy enough to damage hard surfaces.

Unoccupied means that nobody has lived in your home for more than 60 consecutive days.

Section 2 - What is covered

We cover sudden and accidental physical loss of or damage to your buildings and contents caused by the following events.

2.1 Fire, smoke, explosion, lightning and earthquake.

2.2 Storm, including damage to roofs, gutters and windows. Damage to fences, gates and hedges caused by storm is covered up to 2,000 USD per claim.

2.3 Escape of water, including the cost of finding the leak and repairing the wall, floor or ceiling that had to be opened to reach it. The pipe, tank or appliance that failed is not itself covered.

2.4 Theft or attempted theft, provided there was forced and violent entry into the home. Jewelry is covered up to 1,500 USD in total per claim unless a higher limit is shown in your schedule.

2.5 Accidental breakage of fixed glass in windows, doors, skylights and conservatories, including the cost of boarding up an opening until the glass can be replaced.

2.6 Falling trees and branches, including the cost of removing the fallen tree or branch when it has damaged the buildings.

Section 3 - What is not covered

3.1 Flood, unless the flood add-on is shown in your schedule. Escape of water from inside the home is not flood and is covered under 2.3.

3.2 Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, rot, damp and mould, and any damage that happens gradually over time.

3.3 Damage caused by a lack of maintenance. If a pipe bursts because it was known to be corroded and was not repaired, we pay for the resulting water damage only once, and we may decline later claims from the same cause.

3.4 Any loss while your home is unoccupied, other than fire, lightning and earthquake.

3.5 Damage caused deliberately by you or by anyone living with you.

3.6 Damage to swimming pools, hot tubs and their covers caused by frost.

Section 4 - Deductibles

The deductible is the part of each claim that you pay yourself. We deduct it from the amount we pay. The deductible depends on the cause of the damage:

  Cause of damage                                  Deductible per claim (USD)
  Standard (all causes not listed below)           500
  Glass breakage (Section 2.5)                     100
  Storm (Section 2.2)                              1,000
  Escape of water (Section 2.3)                    750
  Theft (Section 2.4)                              250
  Earthquake (Section 2.1)                         2 percent of the buildings sum insured

If one event causes damage that falls under more than one cause, only the highest deductible applies.

Section 5 - How to make a claim

5.1 Report the claim to us within 30 days of the incident, by phone, in the AnyCompany app or on our website.

5.2 Take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, for example by switching off the water supply or covering a broken window. We pay the reasonable cost of these emergency measures.

5.3 Photograph the damage and upload the photos within 7 days of reporting the claim.

5.4 For theft, report the loss to the police within 48 hours and give us the crime reference number.

5.5 Keep receipts, invoices and quotes for the repair or replacement.

5.6 Keep all damaged items until our adjuster has inspected them or has confirmed in writing that you may dispose of them. Important: if damaged items are thrown away before inspection, we may be unable to verify the loss and we may decline the part of the claim that relates to those items. This applies even to items that are wet, smell or are a hazard - move them outside or to a garage instead.

5.7 An adjuster contacts you within 2 business days of the report. For claims below 2,500 USD we may settle without a visit, based on your photos and quotes.

Section 6 - Temporary accommodation

If your home cannot be lived in because of damage covered by this policy, we pay for temporary accommodation of a similar standard for you, your family and your pets. We pay for up to 30 nights in total per claim and at most 150 USD per night for a hotel, or the reasonable cost of a rented home of a similar size. We also pay reasonable extra travel costs to work and school. Please agree the accommodation with your adjuster before you book it, where possible.

Section 7 - Settlement

7.1 For buildings we pay the cost of repair, or of rebuilding when repair is not possible, up to the buildings sum insured.

7.2 For contents we replace the item as new, except clothing, which we pay for after a deduction for wear and tear.

7.3 We pay 80 percent of the covered damage above the deductible for any item without proof of ownership, such as a receipt, photo or bank statement.

7.3.2 Where an item belongs to a pair or set, for example one of two matching earrings, we pay only for the lost or damaged item and not for the whole pair or set.

7.4 We pay within 10 business days of agreeing the settlement amount with you.
