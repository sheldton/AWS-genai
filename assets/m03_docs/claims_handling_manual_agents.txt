AnyCompany Insurance - Claims Handling Manual for Contact Center Agents

Internal guidance, version 4.2, valid from 1 March 2026. Use this manual together with the Home Policy Wording HP-2026.

Status codes in the claims system

Every claim carries one status code. Read the code to the customer in plain words, never as the code itself.

- R1 - Registered. The claim has been recorded. No adjuster assigned yet.
- R2 - Adjuster assigned. Tell the customer the adjuster will call within 2 business days.
- R4 - Waiting for documents. Photos, receipts or a police reference are missing. Tell the customer exactly which ones.
- R7 - Suspended pending fraud review. Do not discuss the reason with the customer. Say that the claim needs an additional review and that a specialist team will contact them within 10 business days. Never mention fraud.
- R9 - Settled. Payment has been released. It reaches the customer's bank within 3 business days.
- X2 - Declined. The decline letter explains the reason. Offer the complaints procedure if the customer disagrees.

Escalation rules

Escalate to a team leader when the customer asks for a manager, when a claim has been at R4 for more than 30 days, or when the customer mentions a vulnerable situation such as a medical condition, a disability or a newborn baby at home. Vulnerable customers can be offered emergency accommodation immediately, before the adjuster visit.

Payments and refunds

Agents may approve emergency payments up to 500 USD for boarding up, drying equipment or emergency locksmiths, without a team leader. Anything above 500 USD needs a team leader. Double charges of the premium are refunded by the billing team within 5 business days; create a billing ticket of type BIL-DUP.

Common questions

Customers often ask whether they may clear out water-damaged rooms. Point them to Section 5.6 of the policy wording: they should keep damaged items until the adjuster has seen them, but they may move them outside.

Customers often confuse escape of water and flood. A burst pipe inside the home is escape of water and is covered. Water from a river or from heavy rain entering from outside is flood and needs the flood add-on.
